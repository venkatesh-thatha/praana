Introducing Praana

A Health System
in Your Pocket

Not a doctor replacement. A doctor-level preparation tool — for every Indian, in every language.

  • Describe symptoms in voice or text → AI cross-references ICD-11 (WHO) → ranked condition shortlist with urgency
  • Upload anything — lab report, X-ray, skin photo, food label, medication — instant plain-language AI analysis
  • Log daily — vitals, meals, exercise → anomaly detection, 7-day trend tracking, Indian nutrition data (IFCT 2017)
  • Profile-aware — every analysis talks to your blood type, conditions, medications, and allergies
  • Doctor Brief — one-tap PDF summary that makes every consultation 10× more productive
Feature 01

From "Something Feels Off"
to Ranked Answers

ICD-11 Grounded
The same disease classification system used by WHO and 115 countries worldwide
  • Speak or type symptoms in Hindi, Tamil, or English — Praana understands all three
  • AI maps to ICD-11 and returns a ranked shortlist: EmergencySee GP SoonSelf-Monitor
  • Each result shows: what to watch for, and exact questions to ask your doctor
  • Voice input via Web Speech API — works fully in-browser, no external service needed
  • Red Flag Detection — emergency symptoms trigger an instant 112 call screen, no delay

Feature 03

Daily Habits, Tracked Against
What Your Body Needs

  • Log HR, BP, SpO2, temp, weight → 7-day sparklines + anomaly alerts
  • Diet log uses IFCT 2017 — India's national food database. Idli, dal, roti are first-class entries, not afterthoughts
  • Nutritional gaps surfaced against your age, weight, conditions, activity
  • Exercise + Yoga recommendations calibrated to your specific health conditions
  • Ayurvedic herbs database with personalised remedy suggestions tied to your symptoms
IFCT 2017
India's national food composition database — the most comprehensive Indian food dataset available. Praana is one of the first health apps to integrate it.
Anomaly Detection
Automatic alerts when 7-day trends deviate from your personal baseline — not population averages.
Feature 04 — Killer Feature

The Doctor Brief
Changes Every Consultation

Average Indian GP consultation: 2 minutes.
Praana makes those 2 minutes count.

  • One-tap PDF: symptom timeline, lab flags, vitals trend, medications, allergies, current concerns — in clean clinical language
  • Doctor spends zero time extracting history — all their time goes to diagnosis
  • Sharable via WhatsApp, email, or print — works in every clinic, no app on the doctor's side needed
  • Powered by Claude AI writing structured medical summaries from your entire health history
  • Especially powerful for specialist referrals, second opinions, and chronic condition management
